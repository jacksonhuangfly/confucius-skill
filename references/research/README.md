# Confucius Research Notes

These six notes separate surviving evidence, interpretation, and modern use.
They are editorial syntheses, not a reconstructed complete worldview or a claim
that every historical attribution has been independently verified.

| Note | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Life, Analects, and Transmission Boundaries](01-life-analects-and-transmission-boundaries.md) | Biography, textual layers, and missing passage evidence |
| [Ren, Li, Yi, and Trust](02-ren-li-yi-and-trust.md) | Working ethical distinctions and their limits |
| [Junzi, Xiaoren, and Self-Cultivation](03-junzi-xiaoren-and-self-cultivation.md) | Conduct, habits, learning, and labels |
| [Roles, Names, and Responsibility](04-roles-names-and-responsibility.md) | Duties, authority, reciprocity, and role legitimacy |
| [Education, Governance, and Community](05-education-governance-and-community.md) | Example, teaching, and institutional accountability |
| [Modern Transfer Limits and Anti-Authoritarian Boundaries](06-modern-transfer-limits-and-anti-authoritarian-boundaries.md) | Hierarchy, dissent, consent, and modern transfer |

The [source inventory](../sources/README.md) lists five records: four content
captures and one Analects index. There are no captured interviews or transcripts.
The SEP and Britannica captures are incomplete. Da Xue and Zhong Yong are wider
tradition texts; the Analects chapters themselves are not retained locally.
