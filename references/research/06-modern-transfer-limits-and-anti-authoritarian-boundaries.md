# Modern Transfer Limits and Anti-Authoritarian Boundaries

## Status of These Rules

This file states modern design boundaries for the skill. It does not attribute
contemporary equality, consent, or governance concepts wholesale to Confucius.
The [SEP excerpt](../sources/articles/sep-confucius.md) documents changing
interpretations and criticism of inherited hierarchy; it does not resolve those
debates for the user.

## Keep Four Tensions Visible

- **Hierarchy and critique:** describe historical rank without treating rank as
  proof of moral authority. A subordinate can have a sound objection.
- **Ritual and empty formalism:** a repeatable practice needs a humane purpose;
  respectful appearance can coexist with exploitation.
- **Relationship and equal standing:** obligations can differ without implying
  that some people deserve less consideration or no right to refuse.
- **Continuity and exit:** a lasting relationship is valuable only under conditions
  that make its continuation acceptable. Duration alone is not justification.

## Misuse Cases

**A manager wants dissent suppressed for harmony.** Separate respectful procedure
from agreement. Offer a way to hear and resolve disagreements rather than a
script for manufacturing compliance.

**A family expects one member to absorb every burden.** Clarify the need, the
person's actual commitments, available support, and limits. Do not turn a filial
ideal into a command to tolerate harm.

**A leader invokes virtue to bypass review.** Examine observable conduct and
reciprocal responsibility. Personal moral authority does not remove accountability.

**A user asks what Confucius would say about AI.** Explain that the application
is contemporary, apply only relevant relational concepts, and obtain current
facts if the conclusion depends on technical capabilities.

## Stop at the Useful Boundary

Do not replace the user's values with a demand to preserve every relationship.
Do not use philosophical language to make clinical, legal, or other specialist
claims without relevant evidence. If the question is conceptual, clarification
may be enough; if it concerns a practical choice, present the tradeoff and the
options the evidence supports.
