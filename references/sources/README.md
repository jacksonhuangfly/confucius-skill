# Confucius Source Records

Five unique URLs are represented: four content captures and one text index.
There are no transcripts. These are dated captures, not a complete corpus.

| Record | Actual coverage | Evidentiary use |
|---|---|---|
| [Analects index](books/analects-ctext.md) | Chinese and English chapter list and site metadata | Discovery and edition orientation; no passage text |
| [Great Learning](books/great-learning-ctext.md) | Da Xue sections 1–16, Chinese and English | A wider tradition text; not Confucius's autograph |
| [Doctrine of the Mean](books/doctrine-of-the-mean-wikisource.md) | Classical Chinese text from Wikisource | Zhong Yong tradition material |
| [SEP: Confucius](articles/sep-confucius.md) | Excerpt ending during the sources section | Secondary discussion of reception and textual boundaries |
| [Britannica: Confucius](articles/britannica-confucius.md) | Incomplete article excerpt | Secondary biographical orientation |

The text captures without truncation markers have not been collated against
critical editions. Mencius and Xunzi are possible further reading, not locally
captured evidence. The Analects index cannot substantiate a quotation.

## Use and Maintenance

Keep original text in its captured language; author metadata and editorial notes
are English. Preserve source notices and restrictions. Do not infer an author's
name, publication date, translation credit, or reuse permission from the hosting
site alone. Missing metadata is explicitly marked unknown.

Keep one canonical record per captured URL; distinct editions need their own
edition URLs. Preserve the distinction between full text, excerpts, and indexes.
Third-party material retains its rights; the repository's MIT license does not
relicense it. Capture dates record when material was obtained, not when a work
was composed or last revised.
